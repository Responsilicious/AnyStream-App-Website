Features

Designed for the sofa, built around your library.

Everything in AnyStream is built for a TV remote: big, clear focus, short paths and no typing where it can be avoided.

  • Jellyfin, Emby and Plex

    Save as many servers as you like and switch between them from the server menu. Each keeps its own account and viewing progress.

  • Pick up where you left off

    Continue Playing, Up Next and Recently Added keep your current films and episodes on the Home screen.

  • Filters and sorting

    Filter movies and series by genre, watch status and year. Sort by title, date added, release date or rating.

  • Search every server at once

    One search covers all your servers. A title found on more than one shows once, marked “On 2 servers”.

  • Look before you play

    Movie pages with cast and synopsis, series pages with every season and episode, and what you have already watched.

  • Plays what you have

    Direct play when your Fire TV can decode the file, including 4K HEVC, and a server stream when it can’t. Choose audio and subtitles in the player.

Set-up guide

From sofa to screen in about a minute.

You need a Fire TV, the AnyStream app, and a Jellyfin, Emby or Plex server you can already sign in to. Watch the walkthrough, or follow the steps below.

  1. 1

    Install AnyStream

    On your Fire TV, search for AnyStream in the Appstore, or open the AnyStream page on Amazon on your phone or computer and send it to your device. It’s free and has no ads.

    Open the app and choose Connect to server.

  2. 2

    Pick your server type

    Choose Jellyfin, Emby or Plex. You can add the others later from the server menu.

  3. 3

    Jellyfin or Emby: enter your server’s address

    Type the address with the on-screen keyboard, including any port, for example 192.168.1.20:8096 or media.example.com. On your home network, Find servers on this network can fill it in for you.

    To watch away from home, use your server’s https:// address.

  4. 4

    Jellyfin: sign in with Quick Connect

    Choose Continue to sign in, then Use Quick Connect. A six-digit code appears on your TV.

    In Jellyfin on your phone or computer, open Settings → Quick Connect, enter the code and authorise it. AnyStream signs in by itself. Prefer a password? Choose Use username & password.

    Quick Connect needs to be turned on for your server. A Jellyfin administrator can switch it on in the Dashboard, under General.

  5. 5

    Emby: pick your user and sign in

    After the address, choose your user from the list (or Other user to type a name), then enter your Emby password. Emby Connect sign-in isn’t supported yet, so use your server account.

  6. 6

    Plex: link with a code

    Choose Sign in with Plex. A four-character code appears. On your phone or computer, go to plex.tv/link, sign in and enter the code. Then pick your server and, if you use Plex Home, your profile.

    AnyStream never sees your Plex password.

Good to know: AnyStream plays the personal media you already have. It does not supply films or programmes, television subscriptions or server hosting. To add another server later, open the server menu at the top of Home and choose Add server.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does AnyStream cost?

Nothing. AnyStream is free on the Amazon Appstore, with no ads and no subscription.

Which media servers does AnyStream support?

Jellyfin, Emby and Plex. Jellyfin signs in with Quick Connect or a username and password, Emby with a username and password, and Plex by linking your account at plex.tv/link.

Which devices does it run on?

Amazon Fire TV devices running Vega OS, such as the Fire TV Stick 4K Select, and Fire TV devices running Fire OS. Both are on the same Amazon Appstore listing. Playback of particular formats depends on your device and server.

Can I save more than one server?

Yes. Add as many Jellyfin, Emby and Plex servers as you like and switch between them from the server menu. Search looks across all of them at once.

Each server keeps its own account and its own viewing progress. AnyStream does not combine or synchronise progress between different servers.

Can I watch away from home?

Yes, if your server can be reached from outside your home network. Use your server’s https:// address when you add it. Plex servers are found through your Plex account.

Do I need my own media library?

Yes. AnyStream is a player for personal media servers you already have access to. It does not provide films, programmes, subscriptions or server hosting.

Does AnyStream change how my Plex account works?

No. AnyStream connects through your existing Plex account, so Plex’s own access and remote-playback requirements still apply. Depending on how your server is set up, watching away from the server’s home network may need a paid Plex plan. Please check Plex’s current terms.
