How it works

Three steps from sofa to screen.

  1. 1

    Connect your library

    Sign in to the Jellyfin or Plex server you already use.

  2. 2

    Choose your server

    Pick the saved library you want to browse, and switch whenever you like.

  3. 3

    Find your next watch

    Browse rows, check the details and press Play.

Good to know: AnyStream plays the personal media you already have. It does not supply films or programmes, television subscriptions or server hosting.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which media servers will AnyStream support?

Support for Jellyfin and Plex is planned. Exact server versions and features will be confirmed nearer to release.

Which devices will it run on?

AnyStream is being designed for Amazon Vega OS. Compatibility is planned rather than confirmed, and we will share details of supported devices when they are known. Playback of particular formats will depend on your device and server.

Can I save more than one server?

Yes, that is the plan. You will be able to add multiple Jellyfin and Plex servers and switch between your saved libraries.

Each server keeps its own account and its own viewing progress. AnyStream does not combine or synchronise progress between different servers.

When will AnyStream be available?

AnyStream is in development and there is no release date yet. Sign up for launch updates and we will let you know when there is news.

Do I need my own media library?

Yes. AnyStream is a player for personal media servers you already have access to. It does not provide films, programmes, subscriptions or server hosting.

Does AnyStream change how my Plex account works?

No. AnyStream connects through your existing Plex account, so Plex’s own access and remote-playback requirements still apply. Depending on how your server is set up, watching away from the server’s home network may need a paid Plex plan. Please check Plex’s current terms.
